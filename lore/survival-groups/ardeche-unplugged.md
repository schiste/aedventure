## Origin story

The Ardèche group reached prepared caves through Activation Protocol Alpha on
Day 0. Its story is not the universal model of survival: it benefited from
advance preparation, known shelters and a pre-existing evacuation network.

The first local observations, the exact fatalities and the first sound test
remain to be reconstructed from Henri Marchand's journal and the Unplugged
archive.
