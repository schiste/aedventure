# The Unplugged of Ardèche

<EntityCard id="group.ardeche-unplugged" />

## Summary

The Ardèche group was prepared before Day 0 through the Unplugged evacuation
network. Its survival is not a template for every refuge: the group benefited
from advance preparation, known caves and an evacuation protocol.

## Origin

On Day 0, the group reached the designated Ardèche caves through Activation
Protocol Alpha. The surviving account attributes the shelter's viability to
its depth, compartmentalized access and continuous sound production.

The exact arrival sequence, the people present and the first local observation
remain to be reconstructed from Henri Marchand's journal and the Unplugged
archive.

## First observations

The group is associated with an early reported observation that continuous
sound was protective against the silence-related illness. This is not yet a
measured threshold. Drums are one documented modality, but they are not assumed
to be the only effective one.

- [Sound-continuity protection](../data/claims.json) is recorded as a shared
  reported claim.
- [Activation Protocol Alpha](../timeline.md) is the associated evacuation
  event.
