# Mponeng Deep Survivors

<EntityCard id="group.mponeng-deep" />

## Summary

The Mponeng group has a different origin from the prepared Ardèche network.
The mine was already occupied when the Filter activated. Its story is one of
accidental underground survival, constrained resources and local discovery.

## Origin

The group formed around workers and families already below ground on Day 0.
The current chronology records an initial population of approximately 1,500;
this remains an editorial estimate pending source-level review.

## First observations

The shared chronology carries a reported claim that continuous sound helped
stabilize people exposed to the new condition. For Mponeng, the available
record establishes underground presence but does not identify a first sound
test. The first observer, first sound source and minimum effective intensity
remain unknown.

This account remains separate from Ardèche. Similar observations may have been
made independently in many refuges, but attribution to Mponeng remains
provisional.

## Later significance

Later lore connects Mponeng with the first Crystal discovery attributed to
Thabo Ndlovu. Keep that history separate from the group's Day 0 origin while
the chronology and discovery date are reconciled.
