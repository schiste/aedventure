## Scope

The world of Aedventure is shaped by two linked disasters: the activation of an orbital acoustic system known as the Filter and the mutation of a synthetic virus later called Hush-2. The Filter makes the unprotected surface lethal. Hush-2 makes sustained sound necessary for most infected humans. These are separate mechanisms and must not be conflated.

The present of the game is **311 AS, or 2345 CE**. Day 0 is **14 October 2034**. Year 1 AS begins on **15 October 2034**.

## Operation Lullaby

Operation Lullaby was a conspiracy intended to reduce disruptive noise through two coordinated systems:

- an orbital network of approximately 340 satellites, designed to dampen selected sound frequencies;
- a synthetic virus designed to induce sound sensitivity without killing its hosts.

The operation was not designed to create Crystals. Crystals are an emergent consequence of the Filter and were not part of the original plan.

The group behind the operation formed around the Telegram community *Sleepless in Decibels*. Its key technical figures became known as the Sounding Five. Their intentions, responsibilities and individual fates are documented in the historical sections of [the original truth file](the-truth.md), the Telegram archives and the timeline. Those sections remain source material and have not yet been fully reclassified.

## The Silence

The original virus mutated shortly before the Filter activated. The resulting strain, Hush-2, affects the brainstem's auditory processing centres. In an infected human, continuous sound can suppress or delay the progression of the disease. Prolonged silence can lead to neurological damage, hallucinations, loss of motor control, coma and death.

The exact sound threshold, the duration of safe silence and the biological mechanism remain incompletely documented. The structured claim for sound protection is therefore `reported`, not an unrestricted mechanical law:

- source: [data/claims.json](data/claims.json);
- status: `reported`;
- modalities reported to help: drums, machinery, water, voice and music;
- minimum intensity: `unknown`.

Sound protects against Hush-2. It does **not** protect a human from the Filter on the open surface.

## The Filter

The Filter is the orbital system's catastrophic failure mode. After the cascade, the open surface became lethal to ordinary humans. The exact physical interaction between the satellite field, airborne sound and living tissue is still described differently by surviving communities, but the operational rule is fixed:

> **The unprotected surface is lethal.**

Depth and rock provide the primary protection. The first survivors therefore lived in caves, mines, bunkers, metro systems and other enclosed underground environments. The initial survival of humanity was exclusively subterranean.

The established underground depth guidance remains:

- **50 metres:** minimum practical threshold;
- **100 metres:** reliable habitat depth;
- **150 metres:** suitable for a large colony;
- **200 metres from an opening:** minimum recommended depth for a refuge core;
- **300 to 500 metres:** preferred for sensitive or strategic areas.

These values are engineering guidance, not a guarantee. Local geology, openings, ventilation, power and sound continuity still matter.

## Surface rule and its exception

The phrase `Surface = death` is retained as a shorthand, but its precise meaning is:

> **A human without effective Crystal protection cannot establish a stable life on the surface.**

A functioning Crystal can create a bounded local bubble in which the Filter's effects are reduced and ordinary sound can propagate. This is an exception produced by a specific anomalous structure. It is not a general restoration of the surface and does not make surface travel safe.

The following are therefore not valid alternatives to Crystal protection:

- drums or music alone;
- sound corridors;
- speakers, resonators or industrial noise;
- coastal waves or other naturally loud environments;
- a surface settlement supplied from an underground refuge.

## Crystals

Crystals are emergent resonance anomalies associated with the Filter's standing-wave nodes. They were not part of Operation Lullaby and were not intentionally created by the Sounding Five.

The approved working chronology is deliberately conservative:

- Crystals become materially present around **200 AS**;
- the previous first-discovery date of **2041 CE, or 7 AS**, is no longer canonical and must be removed or reclassified in the timeline;
- early formations are too small or unstable to protect a settlement;
- viable surface bubbles emerge only after later growth and controlled experimentation;
- a Crystal's field is local, fragile and dependent on its condition and resonance.

The exact formation process, the number of Crystals and the date of the first viable bubble remain to be documented. They must not be filled with invented precision.

## Surface Recovery Expeditions

The old revision log used the name "Sacrifice Cities" for a supposed form of surface expansion. That name and that model are no longer retained. The surface remains lethal to an unprotected human, and sound infrastructure cannot create a stable surface settlement.

Some communities nevertheless conduct **Surface Recovery Expeditions**. These are local, deliberate attempts to retrieve resources that cannot be produced or recovered underground. There is no universal protocol: each community chooses its teams, routes, equipment, selection criteria, acceptable losses and return signals. Protocols remain different, but successful practices can spread as regional traditions through contact, trade or copied expedition records.

The equipment is generally light and deliberately non-standardised. Teams may carry measurement tools, markers, extraction tools and a route back to shelter, but no universal kit exists. Noise is not a protection against the Filter. It may be used to keep Hush-2 dormant, but expeditions generally avoid unnecessary noise because it does not make the surface safer.

Typical targets include medicines, industrial parts, preserved food and energy components. An expedition is justified by a specific need, not by a general desire to occupy the surface. The standard survival window is **4 in-world hours for an ordinary human** and **24 in-world hours for a Resilient**. These are baseline limits, not guarantees: exposure, local geology, available protection, route conditions and the person's state can shorten the operational window.

A local leader may exceptionally authorise exceeding the standard window when the expected value is judged to justify the risk. This is not a new safe duration: it is an explicit decision to accept a high probability of death or permanent injury. The person making the decision on site bears responsibility for the call, and communities may disagree about whether the decision was justified.

Some expeditions eventually identify previously unknown Crystal sites. The sequence after discovery is not universal. A community may document a site, test it, protect it, worship it, hide it, trade access to it or fight over it. A group isolated for three centuries may not recognise a Crystal at all, or may interpret it through an entirely different tradition. These discoveries can support limited, late surface recolonisation, but only after a functioning Crystal bubble has been established. Recovery expeditions themselves do not create permanent surface districts.

The historical accounts previously grouped under "Sacrifice Cities" must be reclassified case by case: some may describe underground expansion, some failed expeditions and some exaggerated memories of temporary operations.

## Crystal settlements

Crystal settlements are a separate and later phenomenon. They are bounded installations around functioning Crystals, not the continuation of Surface Recovery Expeditions. The first viable bubble uses **290 AS as a working date**, not as a final canonical date.

## Resilients

Some lineages show extended tolerance to surface exposure or delayed Hush-2 progression. These individuals are called Resilients. They are not immune, and their tolerance does not invalidate the surface rule.

A Resilient may survive longer than an ordinary human in conditions that would otherwise be fatal. This remains a biological exception with uncertain limits, not a population-scale solution. The status and mechanism of individual Resilient characters must be established from their respective sources.

## Population

The current working estimate is approximately **two million humans** in 311 AS. The total is canonical at the level of a worldbuilding estimate; its distribution between underground communities, Crystal bubbles and exposed areas is not yet fixed.

Population tables that assign large stable populations to Sacrifice Cities must therefore be treated as provisional until they are reconciled with the surface rule. No population breakdown should be copied into a reference page as fact before that reconciliation.

## Historical status of the Crystal Wars

The Three Crystal Wars remain part of the reported history of the world, with a provisional date range around **200 to 250 AS**. Their scale, participants and consequences require source-by-source review.

The Tarn conflict is specifically vulnerable to historical mythologising. The surviving epic may describe a great war even if the underlying event was a smaller family conflict. Both the epic account and the reconstructed account must be preserved with their respective statuses.

## Open questions

The following questions remain open and must not be silently resolved in derived pages:

1. What exact event marks the material appearance of the first Crystal around 200 AS?
2. When does the first stable Crystal bubble become viable?
3. Which reports about Sacrifice Cities describe failed attempts, and which describe underground structures incorrectly remembered as surface cities?
4. What are the actual limits of Resilient tolerance?
5. How many Crystals remain active, damaged or dormant in 311 AS?
6. Which population figures are measured, inferred or mythologised?

Until these questions are resolved, the status of the relevant passages is `reported`, `disputed`, `draft` or `unknown`, never silently `canonical`.
